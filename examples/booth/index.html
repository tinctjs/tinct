Booth — live video filters

Serialized recipes on your webcam at 60 fps via imagepipe/live — texture-resident WebGL2, no readback. The headline looks (Comic, Neon, CRT, Prism, Thermal) are custom shader filters defined in this app with defineFilter: ~40 lines each, and they serialize, replay, and run live like any built-in. Snapshots replay the same recipe through the still pipeline at full resolution.

Waiting for camera… if you decline, an animated demo scene steps in.

An imagepipe example — recipes are plain JSON; live(video).pipe(recipe).into(canvas) is the whole integration.
